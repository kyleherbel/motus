# Composite pictures: hubs, tasks at work, pointers and pulled-out figures

A third pass over the reference, made on 2026-11-17 after a first real edit in this style read as thin. The edit used the generic devices one at a time (a row of two logo tiles, two tiles and a line, a short chip row) and left most of the field empty. Watching the eleven reels again at 1 fps, then the units below at every frame, showed what the first two studies under-weighted: most explanatory units in the reference are composites that fill the zone (77 % of the width, 82 % of the graphic zone [X26]), built from a card plus the things around it, and several keep moving in small, meaningful ways while the speaker talks (a list ticks, a bar fills, a dot flows, a figure counts).

Method: frames decoded at native size with the BT.709 limited-to-full conversion of [C01]; positions read on native frames, durations from 15 and 30 fps strips (the strips are kept with the local study, not in the repository). One coder, by eye; each device rests on one or two units, so every rule here is low confidence and is marked with its instances.

## Rules

### [K01] Hub: a card fed by a row of tiles

- What: a dark card on the column in the lower part of the zone; above it a centred row of dark rounded tiles, each holding a product's mark; a thin grey line from under each tile into the top of the card. It acts out "pulls data from", "connects to many", "integrates with".
- Values (clip34@708-764, settled frame 738): four tiles of 159 px (radius about 40 px, 0.25 of the side), centres x 150, 410, 670, 930 (pitch 260), tops at y 70. The mark is about 0.55 of the tile. The card spans the column (x 41 to 1039) from y 529; header row 104 px with the app's mark; a figure of cap height about 112 px under it.
- Confidence: low (one unit, one clip).

### [K02] Hub lines

- Values (clip34@712-738): 3 px grey lines (about #4B4B4B on the field), each an S-curve that starts 70 px under its tile and ends on the card's top edge 230 px lower. The ends are pulled toward the axis: starts 150..930 end at 250..810, a factor of 0.72 about x 540. No arrowheads [X31]. The tiles arrive 2 frames apart after the card (698, 700, 702, 704) with the tile entrance [M04]; the lines are drawn once the tiles have settled (from 712).
- Confidence: low.

### [K03] Hub flow

- Values (clip34@720-752): on "live data" an accent dot of 12 to 14 px leaves the top of each line, the lines 2 frames apart (720, 722, 724, 726), and runs down to the card in about 14 frames, eased. A second wave runs on a later word (746). Dots that arrive change the card: the figure counts and a row is outlined (clip34@764) [X15].
- Confidence: low.

### [K04] Hub state change

- Values (clip34@764): the card's row takes an accent outline and its value counts to the new figure (13 frames, [M14]). The build outlines the whole card (the card has no rows) and lets the figure count on its own word.
- Confidence: low.

### [K05] Hub as "reaching out"

- Not seen in the reference: the dots of [K03] running from the card up to the tiles. Used for "it goes out and does things for you", the reverse direction of the same picture. A build choice, by analogy with the request-and-reply token of [X19].

### [K10] A pointer that clicks

- Values (clip37@408-448, clip34@870-890): a plain arrow pointer, white with a dark rim, about 44 x 64 px, inside a redrawn app card. It glides to a control in about 6 to 8 frames (clip37@440-446) and the control answers when it lands: a pressed state (clip37@418), a tooltip 2 frames later (446-448), a toggle that flips (clip34@880-890). The pointer stays where it clicked.
- Confidence: low (two units, two clips).

### [K11] Where a pointer enters

- Confidence: none (by eye).

### [K12] A figure pulled out of a capture

- Values (clip39@158-188, native frame 178): on a repository capture in the full layout the "3.2k stars" row takes the outline of [G13] on "already" (164), and on the same beat a dark chip pops out beside it with an accent star and the exact count. The chip is about 600 x 158 px, radius about 34, near black with the cards' shadow; the star about 70 px, the figure heavy with a cap of about 68 px. It stands right of the outlined row, vertically centred on it, and overhangs the card's right edge by about 16 px. It arrives with a blur entrance (164-174) while the figure counts from zero to 3,183 over 14 frames, fast at first: 21 (168), 855, 2,704 (172), 2,993, 3,153 (174), 3,171, 3,178 (176), 3,181, 3,183 (178). The exact count replaces the rounded spoken one ("past 3,000") [X17].
- Confidence: low (one unit).

## What changes in the planning guide

- Fill the zone: a scene whose graphic is a short row of tiles, two tiles and a line, or two or three chips leaves most of the field empty; prefer a composite or a large card for any idea that has more than one part.
- Prefer devices that keep working while the speaker explains the work: a task card that ticks, a hub whose dots flow, a pointer that clicks the setting being described.
- Pull the exact figure out of a capture with a chip when the speaker rounds it.
