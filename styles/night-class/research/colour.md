# Colour

Colour of the night-class style measured on all 32 in-style clips (2886.9 s). Every clip was decoded at full resolution with a calibrated path (raw yuv420p planes, BT.709 matrix, limited to full range in numpy). 5819 samples (every 12th frame, every 15th at 30 fps, about 2 per second) were classified from margin statistics into dark graphic field (58.1% of runtime), light graphic field (7.2%) and full-height footage or full-bleed media (34.7%); 0 of 5819 samples mixed a dark and a light margin. Per clip and field type a temporal-median plate was built (median over 7 to 208 samples) and analysed for base colour, texture lattice, fade, gradient and motion; every sample was scanned for red connected components (11,666 components), saturated hues, text-ink cores and flat neutral fills (3175 regions); 343 targeted frames were re-decoded for glow profiles, title and caption inks and green/yellow accents, and 18 element boxes located by eye were measured by code.

Main findings. The dark field is neutral black: 22 of 31 dark plates have base #000000-#020202, 9 are lifted to #030303-#0C0C0C; U=V=128 in all. It carries a faint static texture chosen per video from about eight templates: dot grids (20 clips; pitch 15.1-36.8 px, median 18.2), 1-px line grids (7 clips; 16 x 18 px in 5) or nothing (3 clips). Texture peaks sit 7-37 luma levels above the base (median 18). The plate never moves (1963 of 1970 tracked frames within +-0.5 px) and carries no grain. The light field is #E3E3E3 (11 clips, #E2E2E2-#E4E4E3) with a near-invisible 11.51 x 9.65 px dot texture and a 42.83-px 1-px grid that shows only in an elliptical spot centred near (540, 1110). The accent is one red, #BD0101 (title bars in 14 clips at R 188-190, G/B <= 3; 61% of all red components have R 170-209), flat, without stroke; warmer #CA0202 and pinkish #C5191A variants occur in 7 clips. Red elements are hard-edged by default (402 of 985); 143 (in 23 clips) carry a Gaussian glow (sigma about 36 px). Title-bar glow belongs to the 2026-10-12..22 generation (9 of 13 clips) and almost disappears after (2 of 15). Text ink is pure white #FEFEFE on dark and on footage (31 clips) and near-black #000000-#0A0A0A on light (11 clips); captions are never coloured. Cards are near-black #121212-#1A1A1A, chips mid-grey #3E3E3E, strokes white #FEFEFE (2-4 px) or grey #6C6C6C-#969696. Green (#27AC0F, 24 clips) and yellow (#EBDA0F, 17 clips) are secondary accents. The presenter panel has no stroke, shadow or glow, and no colour grade is detectable on the footage.

Over time: the light field is almost absent before 2026-10-23 (1 of 14 clips, 1.0% of runtime) and common after (8 of 18 clips, 11.7%), where it carries the closing call to action in 6 of 9 light-using clips. Plain-black dark plates appear only in mixed clips from 2026-10-27. Not done: nothing was watched in playback; transitions between dark and light fields and the colour animation of elements were not measured frame by frame (motion dimension); captured screenshots and phone recordings were treated as content, not palette; fonts were not identified.

### [C28] Build every accent from the release palette: electric cobalt where the reference uses red, ice cyan where it uses yellow, each at the relative luminance of the colour it replaces.

- Method: each measured red becomes the colour at hue 224 deg, HSV saturation 0.9, whose WCAG 2 relative luminance equals the measured one; where saturation 0.9 cannot get that bright, the value stays at 1 and the saturation drops. ## Open questions

- What triggers the per-video red variants (#CA0202, #C5191A, #A40001)? A check against the editor's project files or a larger corpus would decide; nothing in the measured content correlates.
- Is the light-field 42.83 px grid spot centred at y 1110 because it is a fixed plate mask, or does it follow the content? Frames with an empty light field below y 1250 (no caption) would decide; none were found.
- Dot and line strength are measured after VP9 at 0.6-0.95 Mbps; the drawn opacity is unknown. A higher-bitrate copy of one clip would decide the exact dot colour and size.
- Whether the dark-field plates are the same asset scaled differently (pitches 16.5, 18.0-18.4, 19.0 px) cannot be decided from pixels; the lattices are regular and phase-stable within each group.
- Green and yellow component statistics include some captured media (thumbnails, UI). A per-element label from the graphics dimension would separate drawn accents from captures.
- Glow amplitude differs by element (red excess 6-90 at 9-16 px); only one sigma (36 px) was fitted to the median profile. Per-element fits on isolated glowing elements would refine it.
- Light-field ink: some videos use dark grey (#1A1A1A-#232323) instead of near-black; whether this marks a secondary title line (e.g. the italic serif part) was not separated.
