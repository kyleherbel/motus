# Motion

Motion was measured on every frame of the 32 in-style clips (70,005 frames, 2,909 s; 23.976, 24, 25 and 30 fps; VP9 decoded with an explicit BT.709 limited-to-full luma conversion). Four full passes were made: whole-frame differences and thumbnails (layout cuts, caption and title zones), full-frame optical flow on 9 clips (validation only, it under-reads fast moves), a half-resolution field-object pass (every frame: ink-weighted centres, boxes, ink) linked into tracks, and full-resolution crops for the title lockup, the presenter panel, the caption band and a reference push. 5,176 object moves were classified (slide, edge push, scale, fade, wipe, line draw; 3,583 kept after fit checks) and fitted with 16 named curves plus free cubic-beziers on 274 events; 36 title lines and one push were fitted at full resolution.

The grammar is cut-based at the shot level and ease-out-based inside a shot. Every layout change (panel, full-height presenter, no-panel graphic) is a one-frame cut (490 cuts), dark/light field switches are cuts in 52 of 55 cases, caption words swap on one frame at full opacity and size (94 % of 4,346 changes; the rest are hand intrusions), and the presenter panel never moves. Inside graphic shots, titles build one word or letter group every 2 frames with a 3-frame fade and a 35 px slide from the right that settles on a steep ease-out (cubic-bezier(0.07,0.66,0.35,0.95), 0.64 s); cards scale in from about 0.6 size; lists rise; graphics are exchanged by a leftward push (reference cubic-bezier(0.33,0.03,0.02,0.95), 1.14 s, 880 px). The house decelerate curve is cubic-bezier(0.15,0.48,0.35,0.95). Nothing idles, nothing overshoots, the dotted background is static, and pushes carry no motion blur.

Variation: 33-40 % of slides and pushes are in-out rather than ease-out; scale-ins got faster after 2026-10-23 (settle95 0.73 s -> 0.28 s) and are faster in mixed light/dark clips; fades, background ramps and no-panel graphics occur only in mixed-family clips from 2026-10-24. The automatic object classes include fragments of larger animations, so per-class shares and densities are medium or low confidence; the full-resolution reference measurements (title slide, push) and the cut/caption/panel/idle results are the strongest numbers.

## Rules

Each rule: what to build, the numbers (median, range, n), the evidence (clip@frame), and the confidence.

### [M01] Every change between the panel layout (graphic field + presenter panel) and the full-height presenter is a hard cut on one frame; never animate a layout change.

- Values: Cuts dark->full 209, full->dark 208, light->full 38, full->light 29 (+6 found by a relaxed test). Mix test alpha (F_t ~ a*F_t-1 + (1-a)*F_t+2): median 0.03; alpha < 0.15 in 99.5 % (dark->full) and 90.9 % (full->dark); the 7 full->dark exceptions are cuts straight into fast entrances, no blend frames seen. 0 animated reflows, zooms or wipes between layouts.
- Evidence: n=490 cuts, 32 clips. e.g. clip40@106, clip04@1414, clip28@1140.
- Confidence: high
