# Verification

After the nine studies, seven independent checkers re-measured every quantity two studies disagreed on, plus the numbers the build depends on, with methods written from scratch (they did not read the studies' scripts or folders). Values are given for all 32 in-style reels and for the newest 17 (24 Oct - 11 Nov, "G2"), the build target.

Verdicts: **confirmed** the first (or only) claim holds within measurement error; **confirmed (other claim)** the second claim holds; **corrected** neither holds and the checker's value is used; **open** undecided (listed at the end for a person to look at).

74 quantities: 50 confirmed, 3 confirmed (other claim), 15 corrected, 6 open.

### 4. Caption onset relative to ASR word start

- Verdict: confirmed
- Claims: T09 (type): median +2.1 frames / +0.088 s; M04 (motion): median +0.8 frames / +0.033 s
- Measured: Onset frame/fps minus the ASR start of the caption's first word. Pooled median: +2.15 frames / +0.089 s (n=7,893 events, 32 clips; IQR +1.0 to +3.4 frames; bootstrap 95% CI of the median 2.11-2.20). G2: +2.12 frames / +0.088 s (n=4,186, 17 clips; CI 2.08-2.19). Per-clip medians 0.48-3.50 frames (median of medians 2.15). 1-word captions only: +2.16 frames. 69% of events fall within +-3 frames.
- Method: A caption change is a change of the centred caption-line mask between consecutive frames, checked on every frame of the caption band. Each segment's middle frame was OCR'd with Apple Vision. Consecutive identical texts were merged, and the token sequence was aligned to the transcript words by order (difflib), not by time. Events more than 1.5 s from their matched word were dropped. Onset time = first frame index / fps (frame start).
- Note: T09 holds. M04's +0.8 was not reproduced: only clip36 (0.48) and clip33 (0.85) have medians that low. Timing from the frame centre would add +0.5 frame, which moves away from M04. Possible source of the gap: the AAC stream starts at pts -5058 samples, a 115 ms priming offset trimmed by the edit list (about 2.8 frames at 24 fps). Any timing pipeline that ignores it shifts the result. ASR times are faster-whisper estimates (0.02 s steps), not forced alignment. Builder: show each caption about 2 frames (about 0.09 s) after the ASR word start, or at the ASR start if the timings are forced-aligned. Evidence: (32 files).
