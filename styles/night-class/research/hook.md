# Hook and ending

Measured the first frames, the opening title build, how long the title stays, the recurring titles in the title slot, and the comment-keyword CTA ending on all 32 in-style clips (24 dark, 7 mixed, 1 light family; 23.976/24/25/30 fps; 2,881 s, 70,519 frames). Methods: an every-frame pass of every clip at 1/4 scale (calibrated BT.709 limited->full decode; white of title glyphs Y=233-234 -> #fdfdfa..#fefefe, calibrated vs default ffmpeg decode differs by 1-3 codes), a full-resolution every-frame pass of the first 4 s of each clip, Apple Vision OCR of the title band at 4 Hz over every clip (with segment edges refined to the frame), a frame-exact entry/exit pass on 393 title-slot segments, every-frame OCR around each spoken CTA (31 clips), audio RMS at both ends, and the ASR transcripts.

Every clip opens on the graphic field over the presenter panel with the voice and the first one-word caption on frame 0. A two-line centred title lockup (top of ink at y~155, bold sans with an italic-serif phrase, red bar or underline on line 2) builds from ~0.08 s: word by word in 21/32 (one word every 2 frames, each fading in over 4 frames, the line re-centring as it grows), the red bar wiping left to right with the text (0.64 s), complete by ~0.9 s. The title is not pinned: in 26/32 it is shown once, for the first spoken sentence (median 4.1 s), and cut out ~0.2 s after that sentence ends, usually into the full-height presenter; in 6/32 it returns, complete, on later graphic segments. The title slot is re-used for ~4 section titles per minute in the same lockup, each cut in or rebuilt in ~0.3 s and cut out.

The ending is a template: on the spoken 'Comment' (+2 frames) the edit cuts to the graphic field and builds 'Comment “Keyword”' / 'To Get The Doc' (or Link, Video, Waitlist) over ~0.6 s above the deliverable graphic and holds it to the last frame (20/26); a 2026-10-24..11-05 variant instead puts the keyword as a large italic-serif caption on the full-height presenter. The video ends on the last word (tail 0.03 s, no fade, no end card). The CTA changed over time: waitlist 'launch' CTA 2026-10-12..10-24 (14 clips) between lead-magnet CTAs (2 before, 15 after).
