# Type

Measured on all 32 in-style clips (24 dark_teaching_graphics, 7 mixed_light_dark_teaching, 1 light_teaching_graphics; 2026-10-10 to 2026-11-11; 70,005 frames at native 23.976/24/25/30 fps). Every frame was decoded with an explicit BT.709 limited-to-full matrix (white glyph cores read 252.7-254.5, black ink 1-2) and reduced to stride-2 text masks of the caption band (y 1150-1469) and the title band (y 30-379). From those masks 8,673 caption events were segmented (bounding box and mask identical frame to frame), read with Apple Vision OCR and aligned to the faster-whisper word timings (median 87 % of ASR words covered per clip, range 61 % clip02 to 93 %). In addition 2,896 native frames (one per second) were OCRed in full and every text line (17,015) was measured natively: cap height, x-height, baseline, face runs (sans or italic serif by stroke/cap ratio), ink and background colour. Fonts were identified by rendering candidates from installed fonts in headless Chrome at fitted size and tracking and scoring overlap with 50 %-threshold masks (whole-line IoU and per-glyph IoU).

Findings. Two faces carry everything: a heavy neo-grotesque sans (best match SF Pro Display Heavy, runner-up Inter 800 at text optical size, x/cap 0.754-0.764 measured on 3,996 lines) and Instrument Serif Italic (IoU 0.70-0.88 against <=0.46 for every other serif, 8 of 9 targets in 6 clips). Captions are one word at a time (85.4 % of 7,724 fully aligned captions; 13.1 % two words, 1.5 % three), white (#FFFFFF) on dark and footage, black on the light field, centred on x=540 with a baseline at y=1306-1307, x-height 32 px in panel mode and 38 px over full-height footage (ratio 1.19), no shadow, outline or box, hard cut in and out, and replaced by the next word on the same frame in 99.8 % of transitions. Words join only inside a phrase: never across punctuation or a pause of 0.10 s or more, mostly short function-word pairs. Named frameworks are shown once as an italic-serif caption, 1.33x taller, in Title Case with a period (37 events, 21 clips). The pinned title is a centred Title Case lockup of one or two lines in the sans with 1-3 key words in the serif at the same cap height, line 2 on a red bar (36 % of 128 unique lockups), over a red underline (28 %) or plain (36 %); it enters with a left-to-right per-character fade-and-track-in (median 13 frames, 0.52 s) and leaves on a cut (90 %). The CTA lockup is 'Comment “Keyword”' with the keyword in the serif inside curly quotes, and from 2026-10-23 a second line 'To Get The Doc/Link/Video' on the red bar; the same words appear as one two-word caption.

Over time the caption system did not change (x-height 30-34 px in 31 of 32 clips across all dates). The CTA moved from plain/underlined (5 clips, Oct 12-19) to the red-bar lockup (6 of 9 clips after Oct 23), and red bars became more common in titles (30 % of unique lockups Oct 10-20, 41 % Oct 21-Nov 11). Two clips are outliers: clip02 (Oct 11, sentence-case title without bar) and clip33 (Nov 4, phrase build-up captions at moving positions with grey pills). Nothing was heard; caption timing is relative to unverified ASR word times. Graphic-internal text was measured only at 1-fps samples and graphics scale while they move, so those sizes are at-rest samples with a wide spread.

## Open questions

- SF Pro Display Heavy vs Inter (text optical size) cannot be separated at 0.6-0.95 Mbps; a clean high-bitrate frame or the creator's project file would decide. Fonts not installed here (Helvetica Now Display Bold/ExtraBold, Satoshi, Geist, General Sans) were not tested.
- What sets the title size per title: neither longest-line width (445-820 px) nor character count is constant. A fit of size against both lines' character counts and the field height of the graphic below might explain it.
- Caption baseline per video (1258-1324 px): possibly placed relative to the panel top or the presenter's head; the layout and presenter analysts' panel geometry per clip would decide.
- Whether the editor's caption grouping is a tool setting (for example 'max N characters, min duration') or manual; the probabilistic rule in T08 reproduces the 15 % join rate but not each decision.
- Per-character title reveal: exact per-glyph fade duration and the track-in spacing curve were not fitted (stride-2 masks plus one native strip).
- Bar corner radius (about 6 px) and the red values are blurred by the codec; the colour analyst's calibrated palette should take precedence for colours.
- Caption detection missed 7-39 % of spoken words per clip (median coverage 0.867, range 0.61-0.93), mostly where captions touch bright graphics; grouping and timing statistics assume the missed captions behave like the detected ones.
