# Example 1: launch news with the maker's footage

The plan used for the style's end-to-end test. The take is one of the repository owner's own Daily Update recordings (68.7 s of 4K landscape, cut to 57.0 s), so the words are real speech: a new ads platform in ChatGPT. The plan validates and renders.

Files: `01-launch-news.words.json` (the conformed words the plan points at) and `01-launch-news.edit.json` (the plan). The assets it names live in the test project, not in the pack: twelve clips cut from the maker's own videos (a 49 s product film and a podcast episode) into `assets/launch/`, and three brand marks from `motus logo --mono`.

## How the take was cut

The recogniser had stretched two words ("its" 1.7 s, "platform" 4.7 s) over two false starts of the opening line. Re-transcribing that stretch on its own found three takes; the cut keeps the last. Every other pause was cut to 0.24 s, and the voice starts on frame 1.

## Against the guide

- 28 cuts a minute (guide 23 to 46); `split` 73 %, `face` 14 %, `full` 13 % (guide about 60 / 17 / 15; the checklist allows `split` up to 70 %, so this plan runs 3 points long on the split).
- Only two sources (the product film and the podcast) plus marks; the guide asks for about five. The maker's pages could not be captured (a bot check), and there is no speaker's own screen recording.
- The split's speaker is larger than the reference's (face 240 px against 172): the owner sits closer to the camera, and the style never shrinks the panel below cover.
- Google's mark holds about 2 s twice (s06, s10); a logo shot in the reference holds 1.8 s median.
